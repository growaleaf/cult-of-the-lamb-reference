Sources & caveats:
  • cultofthelamb.wiki.gg, "Buildings" page (redirected from "Buildings and Decorations"), read via archive.org after the live page returned HTTP 410. Page footer states it was last edited 29 January 2024, and that pages created before February 2024 originate from the Fandom Cult of the Lamb Wiki, now hosted on wiki.gg under CC BY-SA 4.0. This is the primary source for every building row and most decoration rows, and covers the base game plus the Cultist Pack, Heretic Pack, Twitch Unlocks, Merch, Blood Moon Festival, and the four Crusade-area decoration sets (Darkwood, Anura, Anchordeep, Silk Cradle). Its "Sinful Pack DLC" section header had no items listed under it as of this read.
  • TechRaptor, "Cult of the Lamb Decorations Guide", published August 12, 2022 (launch week), read via archive.org after the live site returned HTTP 403. Used to cross-check decoration costs and to supply "Unlocked By" detail (Chest / Challenge Reward / Shop, specific Divine Inspiration tiers) that wiki.gg's page layout does not cleanly expose.
  • TechRaptor, "Cult of the Lamb Divine Inspiration and Building Guide", published August 12, 2022 (launch week), read via archive.org after the live site returned HTTP 403. Used only to corroborate Natural Burial's Doctrine requirement; its launch-week building costs (e.g. "4 Wood, 8 Coin" for Sleeping Bag) are consistently lower than wiki.gg's current figures and are treated as an outdated snapshot, not a same-era conflict, since the game has been patched repeatedly since August 2022.
  • game8.co, gamepressure.com, and thegamer.com (all named as candidate sources in the charter) were searched but did not return a static, citable buildings/decorations list separate from the sources above within this session's search budget; none of their content was used. No calculator or interactive planner for this game's building costs was found anywhere — this page's planner is original, built from the sourced per-item costs above.
Version context: wiki.gg's page (edited January 2024) is treated as the current baseline because it is the most recently updated and most complete of the sources found, covering every DLC and Crusade area. TechRaptor's two guides are an August 2022 launch-week snapshot and are shown only where they corroborate or conflict with wiki.gg, never as a replacement baseline.
Blanks are shown as "not published," never invented, and a genuinely free item (most Paths, two DLC floorings) is shown as "None" rather than merged into "not published." Where two sources disagree on quantity or resource name for the same item, both readings are shown in a flagged note rather than one being silently picked. This is an unofficial fan reference, not affiliated with Massive Monster or Devolver Digital.
